How a move to North Yorkshire works

  1. Check North Yorkshire Police’s current transferee, rejoiner or police staff advert and the ranks and roles it is recruiting for.
  2. Ask your current force for your attendance, performance and discipline record. Most forces ask about these on the application.
  3. Write your examples using the framework in the advert, usually the Competency and Values Framework (CVF).
  4. Pass the interview, vetting, medical and references.
  5. Agree a start date with both forces. Transferees usually move on their current pay point.

Before you apply

  • Specialist and detective posts often need your accreditation to be current, so keep your evidence handy.
  • Many forces prefer rejoiners who left within the last five years. Check the advert for the current rules.

Always rely on North Yorkshire Police’s own advert for eligibility, pay and closing dates.
