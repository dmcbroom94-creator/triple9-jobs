Support & wellbeing

Mental health, crisis lines, charities, federations and unions for police, ambulance, nursing, fire, control room, prison and border staff, serving or retired.

Need to talk to someone now?

If your life or someone else’s is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. Otherwise, these free services are open 24 hours a day and you don’t have to be in crisis to use them.

Wellbeing & mental health

Free, confidential help from charities that understand the job. Many also support retired staff and families.

All services

Blue Light Together (Mind)

For: Emergency services staff, volunteers, retirees, friends and family.

Mental health guides, real stories and advice built by Mind with the emergency services charities. Mind’s Blue Light Programme and its Blue Light Champions network ended in July 2023, but this site and Mind’s workplace support continue.

Visit website
Police

Police Care UK

For: Serving and former police officers, staff, volunteers and their families.

Free, confidential psychological therapy, financial grants and practical help for anyone harmed as a result of their policing role.

0300 012 0030

Visit website
Police

Oscar Kilo

For: Everyone working in policing in England & Wales, families and leavers.

The National Police Wellbeing Service: wellbeing resources, toolkits and the 24/7 Mental Health Crisis Line.

Visit website
Police

Flint House & the Police Treatment Centres

For: Officers and many police staff who donate to them, plus eligible retired officers.

Physical rehabilitation and mental health support at residential centres. Flint House is in Oxfordshire; the Police Treatment Centres are in Harrogate and Auchterarder.

Visit website
Police

Police Firearms Officers Association

For: Firearms officers, others in armed policing, and their families.

Welfare support, counselling, EMDR, coaching and help after incidents, run by serving and retired officers.

Visit website
Ambulance

TASC – The Ambulance Staff Charity

For: Serving and retired ambulance staff, volunteers, students and families.

Mental, physical and financial wellbeing support, plus the 24/7 Ambulance Staff Crisis Line.

0800 1032 999

Visit website
Fire

Fire Fighters Charity

For: Serving and retired fire service staff, including control, and their families.

Physiotherapy, residential rehabilitation, mental health support, bereavement support and the 24/7 Crisis Line.

0800 389 8820

Federations, unions & associations

Thinking of moving forces, going for promotion or leaving? Speak to your rep first. They can advise on pensions, references, misconduct, ill-health retirement and your rights.
