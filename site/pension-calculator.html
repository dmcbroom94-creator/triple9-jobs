How this works, and what it leaves out

  • It's a rough guide, in today's money. Each year you build up a slice of pension (your pay divided by the scheme's rate), and we assume your pay rises in line with inflation.
  • It uses the career average schemes that started in 2014 or 2015. If you have service in an older final salary scheme (the McCloud remedy), that part works differently. Put the figure from your annual benefit statement in "pension built up so far".
  • Leaving before your scheme's normal pension age usually means a deferred pension, paid later. You can often take it earlier with a reduction.
  • It doesn't include ill-health pensions, part-time hours, extra pension you've bought, or the State Pension.
  • For a proper figure, ask your scheme's pension administrator for a quote, or use your scheme's own online tools. This isn't financial advice.
