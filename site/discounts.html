Discounts & offers

Discount schemes and offers for police, fire, ambulance, control room, prison and border staff, serving and retired.

Leaving soon? Sign up while you still have your work email. Most schemes verify you fastest through your police, fire, NHS or prison email. Several also accept retired officers and staff, so check the retired rules before you go.

Discount cards

Most popular

Blue Light Card

£4.99 for 2 years

Who can join: police (including retired), fire (including retired), ambulance, HM Prison & Probation, HM Coastguard, Border Force and Home Office, MoD police and fire, NHS, mountain rescue, RNLI and many volunteers. Retired frontline workers with at least 4 years’ service can also join.

What you get: thousands of online and high street discounts, discounted gift cards, member days and ticket ballots.

Free to join

Who can join: NHS and healthcare staff, plus police (including retired), fire (including retired), ambulance, prison and probation, HM Coastguard, MoD police, mountain rescue, RNLI and other rescue services.

Days out

Boundless

Annual membership, from about £40 a year

Who can join: current and retired emergency services staff, including police, fire, ambulance, HM Coastguard, RNLI and search and rescue, plus the wider public sector.

What you get: free entry to popular attractions, Tastecard, breakdown cover and savings at 180+ brands.

Ex-forces

Defence Discount Service

Free online. Optional in-store card £4.99 for 5 years

Who can join: serving and reserve armed forces, veterans, MoD civil servants and families. Useful if you served in the forces before joining the emergency services.

What you get: online and in-store discounts run by the same company as Blue Light Card.
