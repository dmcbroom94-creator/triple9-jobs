About & contact

Who we are, how we handle your privacy, and how to get in touch.

About Triple 9 Careers

Triple 9 Careers is an independent job board for people serving in, or moving on from, the police, ambulance, fire, control room, prison and border services. We bring together transferee and rejoiner vacancies, police staff roles and civilian jobs that value your skills, alongside free tools: a CV builder, interview guide, pay calculator, police pay scales, a promotion hub and courses for your next step.

Jobs are checked every day and link straight to the employer’s own advert. Always apply through the official advert. We are not part of any police force, NHS trust, fire service, prison service or the Home Office.

Save Triple 9 as an app

Add Triple 9 Careers to your phone’s home screen and it opens like an app, full screen, with our icon. It’s free, there’s nothing to download from an app store and no account needed.

iPhone & iPad

  1. 1Open triple9careers.co.uk in Safari.
  2. 2Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4Tap Add. The Triple 9 icon appears on your home screen.

On newer iPhones you can also do this from Chrome: tap the Share button in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. 1Open triple9careers.co.uk in Chrome.
  2. 2Tap the three dots in the top-right corner.
  3. 3Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. 4Tap Install or Add to confirm.

Using Samsung Internet? Tap the menu (three lines), then Add page to, then Home screen.

Privacy & cookies

No accounts, no sign-up. You never need to register, and we do not ask for your name, email or CV to use the site or post in the Community.

Our tools run on your device. What you type into the CV builder, pay calculator or search box stays in your browser and is never sent to us or saved.

Community posts. If you post in the Community, we store what you write, the nickname you choose (or “Anonymous”) and when you posted, and show it publicly. We don’t ask for your name or email. To stop spam, we keep a scrambled one-way code made from your IP address for 24 hours, then delete it automatically; it can’t be turned back into your IP address and changes every day. If you report a post, we keep the reason you give. Posts stay until a moderator removes them – email info@triple9careers.co.uk with a link to anything you’d like taken down.

No tracking cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) for security, for a short time.

If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it when it is no longer needed. You can ask us what we hold, or ask us to delete it, at info@triple9careers.co.uk.

Links to other sites. Job adverts, courses and discount schemes are on other websites with their own privacy policies. Some links are partner links, which means we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects which jobs we list.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
