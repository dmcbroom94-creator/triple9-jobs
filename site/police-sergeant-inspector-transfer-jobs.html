Moving up or across

Some forces recruit at your current rank (level transfer), while others advertise transfer-on-promotion posts. Our promotion page lists forces running external promotion processes and NPPF exam details.

Before you apply

  • Check whether the post needs a substantive rank or accepts temporary rank.
  • Prepare leadership examples using the CVF level for the rank.
  • Speak to your federation or the Superintendents’ Association about pay and pension.
