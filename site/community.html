Triple 9 Community

A place to ask questions and talk to people who’ve been there – serving, joining or leaving. No sign-up: post with a nickname or as Anonymous.

Ask anything. Share what you know.

No account, no email, no sign-up. Pick a nickname or post as Anonymous. Transfers, interviews, pay, pensions, leaving the job – if you’re wondering about it, someone here has probably been there.

The community board isn’t available here. It runs on triple9careers.co.uk – please try again there in a moment.

Community rules

  • Be respectful. No abuse, harassment, discrimination, threats or bullying.
  • Stay anonymous. Don’t post names, warrant or collar numbers, addresses, phone numbers or anything that identifies you or someone else.
  • Nothing operational. Never discuss live cases, incidents, intelligence, victims, detainees, patients or prisoners. Your service’s social media policy still applies.
  • No adverts, spam or links. Links, emails and phone numbers are blocked automatically.
  • Nothing illegal. Illegal content is removed and may be reported to the authorities.

See something that breaks the rules? Press Report. Posts with several reports are hidden until a moderator checks them. To raise anything else, email info@triple9careers.co.uk. Posts are public, so don’t share anything you wouldn’t want your sergeant, manager or family to read. How we handle forum posts is explained in our privacy notice.
