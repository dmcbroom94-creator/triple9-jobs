1. Two pages, max.
Most recent role first. No photo, date of birth, collar or staff number.

Calls a shift, team size, cases handled, budgets, time saved.
4. Match the advert's words.
Many firms scan CVs by computer, so reuse the advert's keywords honestly.

6. Mind the rules.
Never include operational, sensitive or personal case details.

Jargon buster

Swap service terms for words any employer understands.

PACE UK evidence and procedure law
NDM structured decision-making model
THRIVE risk and vulnerability assessment
Taskings assigned workload
PIP2 accredited investigator
Collar number leave it off your CV
Shout / job incident or call-out
Gold / Silver / Bronze strategic / tactical / operational lead

Answer with STAR

Use this for application questions and interviews.

SSituation: set the scene in one sentence, no jargon.
TTask: what you were responsible for.
AAction: what you did, step by step. Say “I”, not “we”.
RResult: the outcome, with a number if possible.

Example question for a fraud investigator role: Tell us about a time you found key evidence others had missed.
